# Celfocus Digital

Use the Celfocus 2026 corporate palette and Aptos typography, with the official transparent Celfocus vector wordmark and a separate Digital descriptor.

## Identity

The original vector remains graphite on Light and white on Dark. Preserve its proportions. Never redraw it, add a plaque, or enlarge a low-resolution raster as a substitute. The corporate motif is a secondary asset and remains within its native resolution.

## Modes and materials

Dark and Light use identity.json, including the corporate red and neutral ladder. Glass has a translucent fill and blur; Solid has an opaque fill. Use opaque surfaces for tables and forms. Selected controls have an inverse fill and a checkmark.

## Motion and type

Use peripheral arcs, slow light and sparse rising particles. Keep text still; freeze for reduced motion and print and pause hidden documents. Supply licensed Aptos assets from the installation. Segoe UI and Arial are fallbacks.

## Exchange

The shared ds- slide vocabulary supports the three interchangeable identities. Existing corporate templates keep their specialized classes and remain available.
