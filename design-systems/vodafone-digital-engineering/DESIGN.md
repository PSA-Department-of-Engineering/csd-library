# Vodafone Portugal Digital Engineering

Use the established ADC deck v15 identity: Vodafone red, the Vodafone typeface, a near-black technical stage, dual-scale grids, drifting light, rising particles and a three-circle orbit.

## Identity

The original speechmark accompanies Digital Engineering, with VODAFONE PORTUGAL below and the Portuguese flag. Dark uses the white speechmark; Light uses the same silhouette in Vodafone red. Never redraw or replace this lockup with an invented symbol. The assets folder holds the approved speechmark and flag.

## Modes and materials

Dark and Light use identity.json. Glass and Solid are independent of mode. Selected controls must have an inverse filled background and a checkmark in both modes. Dense reading surfaces are opaque.

## Motion and type

Keep ambient motion in backgrounds and connective elements. Text stays still. Freeze on reduced motion and print, and pause hidden documents. Supply the licensed Vodafone face from the installation's private font assets. Use the declared fallback stack where those assets are unavailable.

## Exchange

The shared ds- vocabulary supports the interchangeable themes. The original vodafone-pt-digital catalog theme preserves the ADC shell's vfd- vocabulary for existing decks.
